Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 November 2026. This policy explains how information is handled when you use the QZIX CIRCULAR website and its optional Project Updates form.

Information collected through the form

If you choose to submit the Project Updates form, the website collects your name, email address, consent selection and the submission time. The form is optional. Do not submit bank details, card details, UPI PINs, passwords, identity documents or one-time passwords.

How the information is used

The information is used only to respond to your request for project information and to maintain a record of that request. It is not used to accept a booking, purchase, investment or payment. QZIX does not intentionally sell this form information or use it for advertising.

Storage and service providers

Form submissions are stored in a database hosted by Supabase, which provides the database service used by this website. Access to submitted requests is restricted through the website's administrative access controls. The website itself is hosted through GitHub Pages. These providers may process technical information necessary to provide and secure their services.

Sharing and retention

Information may be disclosed where required by law or where reasonably necessary to protect the website, its users or the project. Requests are retained only as needed to respond and maintain appropriate administrative or security records. A specific deletion schedule has not been published.

Your requests

To ask about, correct or request deletion of information you submitted, contact the project using the email below. Some information may need to be retained where required by law or for legitimate security records.

Contact

Privacy questions: qzixcoin@gmail.com.

QZIX CIRCULAR is currently an informational concept. Online participation, payment collection, ownership records, resale, withdrawals and payouts are not enabled.
