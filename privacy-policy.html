Privacy Policy

This policy explains how information may be handled when you visit the QZIX CIRCULAR website or contact the project.

If you contact the project by email, the information you include may be used to respond to your enquiry. The current website is an informational concept and does not offer online participation, payment collection or account-based transaction services.

The website host may process basic technical information such as browser requests, timestamps and security logs to deliver and protect the website. The project does not intentionally request bank passwords, UPI PINs, card security codes or one-time passwords.

Information sent to the support email is used to respond to the request and maintain appropriate project records. Information is not intentionally sold. It may be disclosed where required by law or to protect the website and its users.

Retention and requests

You may contact the project to ask about personal information you have sent by email. Some records may need to be retained for legal, security or administrative reasons.
