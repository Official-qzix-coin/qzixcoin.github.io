Privacy Policy

Important: This page describes the intended privacy rules for the website. The project operator must confirm the actual data flows, service providers, retention periods and contact details before accepting real buyer registrations or payments. Publishing this page alone does not mean those controls are technically active.

1. Information a buyer may be asked to provide

If buyer registration is enabled, the requested details are:

  • Full name
  • Mobile number
  • Email address

The website may also process information needed to operate the service, such as a token ID, registration or ownership status, transaction reference, timestamps, and basic technical or security logs. Do not submit passwords, bank PINs, UPI PINs, card security codes or OTPs to QZIX COIN.

Information may be used to create and manage a buyer account or request, identify a buyer in relation to a token, provide ownership or verification records, respond to support requests, maintain security, prevent fraud, and meet applicable legal obligations. We will not use contact details for unrelated promotional messages without an appropriate lawful basis or consent where required.

3. Sharing and service providers

Personal information is not intended to be sold. It may be accessible to authorized project administrators and to hosting, database, authentication, security or other service providers only where needed to operate and protect the website. Information may also be disclosed where required by law, a valid legal process, or to address security or fraud concerns. The operator should identify the actual providers and configure their access appropriately.

4. Storage, security and retention

Reasonable technical and organizational safeguards should be used to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, loss or disclosure. No website or internet transmission can be guaranteed completely secure. Information should be retained only for as long as needed for the stated purposes, record-keeping, dispute resolution or legal requirements, and then securely deleted or anonymized where feasible.

5. Your choices and requests

You may contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information, or to withdraw consent where applicable. Some records may need to be retained where required by law or for legitimate transaction and security records. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request.

6. Cookies and technical data

The website or its service providers may use essential browser storage, cookies or technical logs for sign-in, security, preferences and service operation. Browser settings may allow you to control cookies, but disabling essential storage may affect website features. Any analytics or advertising technologies, if introduced, should be disclosed here before use.

This website is not intended for children to submit personal or payment information independently. A parent or legal guardian should contact us if a child’s information has been submitted and they want it reviewed or removed, subject to applicable legal requirements.

We may update this policy when website features, legal requirements or data practices change. The revised version will be posted on this page with an updated date. Please review it periodically.
