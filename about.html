About QZIX CIRCULAR

QZIX CIRCULAR is a proposed circular participation concept. This website currently provides project information, illustrative price levels and a percentage calculator only.

How to read the examples

Displayed references such as ₹20 · 1% and ₹40 · 1%, and example circular values such as ₹2,000 and ₹4,000, are illustrations. They do not establish a legal share, ownership interest, investment entitlement, repayment right or guaranteed value.

Participation status

Online participation, payment collection, ownership records, resale, withdrawals and payouts are not enabled. A displayed amount or percentage must not be treated as an invitation to pay or as confirmation of participation.

No guarantee

No profit, return, appreciation, liquidity, resale value, redemption or future price is promised or guaranteed.

Legal review before launch

Any future real-money activity will require appropriate legal, tax, consumer-protection and payment-provider review, together with clear written terms and operational safeguards. The project will not treat a calculator result as proof of a financial or ownership right.
